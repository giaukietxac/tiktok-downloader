Step-by-Step: Download TikTok Videos to iPhone Camera Roll

1 Copy the TikTok Link

Open the TikTok app on your iPhone. Locate the video you wish to download, tap the Share button (arrow icon on the right side), and select Copy Link.

2 Paste the URL into TikSnap

Open Safari (or Chrome) on your iPhone, go to tiksnap.space, paste your link into the search box, and tap the Download button.

3 Save Clean Video to Photos (Camera Roll)

Tap Download HD Video (No Watermark). A popup in Safari will ask "Do you want to download...?", tap Download.

💡 How to move to Photos: Tap the blue Download icon () in Safari's address bar → tap the video → tap the Share icon at the bottom left → select Save Video. The video is now permanently in your iPhone's Photos album!
